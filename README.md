# Calm Tide 🐋

Daily positive affirmations spoken aloud, a guided breathing meditation, and calming background sounds including whale song.

**Open the app:** https://ranjanielango033wins.github.io/calm-tide/

## Features
- A new affirmation every day, read aloud in a soft, soothing voice (choose voice and speed)
- Every spoken affirmation is shown on screen as text
- "Repeat 3 times" mode for affirmation practice
- Breathing meditation (breathe in 4, hold 4, breathe out 6) with a timer of 3 to 20 minutes
- Affirmations spoken softly during meditation every 30, 60 or 90 seconds
- Sound mixer: whale song, ocean waves, soft rain and a singing bowl, each with its own volume
- Add your own affirmations and save favourites
- Installable as a mobile app on Android and iPhone, and works offline once installed

## Install on your phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**

Keep the screen on during meditation, as phones may pause sound when the screen locks.
